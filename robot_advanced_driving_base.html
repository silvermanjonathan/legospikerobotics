The wheel

One full turn of the wheel moves the robot 27.63 cm. LEGO's Python programs for this robot give that figure in a comment. LEGO's Python programs (PDF).

Before the first session, build the reference robot and check every row of the port map against it. Sessions 1 and 2 use only ports A and E. Sessions 3 and 4 use one colour sensor; their programs name port B as a first try, and each team replaces it with the letter on its own robot.

LEGO stopped selling SPIKE Prime on 30 June 2026 and supports the SPIKE App until 30 June 2031 (LEGO Education). Keep a spare of every part this robot needs, and an unpublished copy of the five building books.
