The test field

Every line, its distance in cm, and where the robot starts.

The rules of the field

  • A pale, plain, flat floor or table. Every line is black tape.
  • The robot starts behind the start line with its front edge on the line, facing forward. It starts there on every run.
  • Every distance is in cm, measured with a ruler from the start line to the robot's front edge.
  • A wheel marker is a small piece of tape on the floor beside a wheel, put down before the first run.

Sessions 1 and 3: the lane

A start line and a far line. The far line is 55 cm from the start line. In session 3 it is lifted and moved to 40 cm and 70 cm as well.

The lane from above, drawn to scale, with the far line at 55 cm.

Session 2: the turn cross

Two strips of black tape that cross at a right angle, like a plus sign. The robot starts over the middle, facing along strip 1. After a quarter turn it faces along strip 2.

The turn cross from above.

Session 2: the square

The robot drives four sides and four quarter turns to its right. In chunk 3 each side is about 28 cm. In the mission each side is about 55 cm and a crate no more than 10 cm across sits in the middle.

The mission square from above, drawn to scale.

Session 4: the delivery run

The lane, with the far line somewhere from 40 to 70 cm from the start line, and a drop zone: a square of tape 40 cm on each side, to the right of the far line.

The delivery run from above, drawn to scale with the far line at 55 cm.

Sessions 7 and 8: the line and its stop bar

Teacher panel

The drop zone is laid from the reference robot: run session 4's chunk 1 program with the far line at 55 cm and centre the square of tape on where the wheels end. Its place in the figure is a sketch of that, not a measurement.

The robot outline in every figure is not to scale. The lines, the square, and the scale bars are.
