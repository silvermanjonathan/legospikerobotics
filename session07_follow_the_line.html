What is new today

Question: What does the motion sensor count, and in what size of step?

How far the hub has turned since the count was set to 0, in tenths of a degree of the robot's turn.

Question: Why did the measured turn end a little past the angle it waited for?

The wheels were still turning when the test became true, and they took a moment to stop.

Question: Your team compared two ways of turning. How did you decide which was better?

By running each one three times from the same start and measuring how far the wheels ended from their markers.

Question: A reading is less than DARK. Is the sensor over the tape or over the floor?

Over the tape. Black tape sends less light back, so it gives the smaller number.

Chunk 1 of 3

Choose between two things: if and else

Line 10 is a test. If the reflected light is less than DARK, the hub runs line 11 and skips line 13. If it is not, the hub skips line 11 and runs line 13. It never runs both.

Line 11 is inside the if line and line 13 is inside the else line, so each starts four more spaces in. Line 14 is back at four spaces. It runs either way and keeps the letter on the light matrix for 3 seconds.

from hub import light_matrix, port
import color_sensor
import runloop

LINE_SENSOR = port.B
DARK = 50


async def main():
    if color_sensor.reflection(LINE_SENSOR) < DARK:
        light_matrix.write("T")
    else:
        light_matrix.write("F")
    await runloop.sleep_ms(3000)


runloop.run(main())

Chunk 2 of 3

Keep choosing: while

To follow a line, the robot follows its edge. Over the tape, it steers a little to the right, off the tape. Over the floor, it steers a little to the left, back onto the tape. It wiggles along the right edge.

One choice is not enough. Line 16 is a while line: it runs lines 17 to 20 again and again, for as long as its own test is true. Its test reads the other colour sensor, the stop sensor. When the stop sensor reaches the stop bar, the test is false, the repeating ends, and line 21 stops the wheels.

from hub import port
import color_sensor
import motor_pair
import runloop

LINE_SENSOR = port.B
STOP_SENSOR = port.F
DARK = 50


async def main():
    # The left drive motor is in port A. The right drive motor is in port E.
    motor_pair.pair(motor_pair.PAIR_1, port.A, port.E)

    # Follow the edge of the line until the stop sensor reaches the stop bar.
    while color_sensor.reflection(STOP_SENSOR) > DARK:
        if color_sensor.reflection(LINE_SENSOR) < DARK:
            motor_pair.move(motor_pair.PAIR_1, 30, velocity=150)
        else:
            motor_pair.move(motor_pair.PAIR_1, -30, velocity=150)
    motor_pair.stop(motor_pair.PAIR_1)


runloop.run(main())

The other edge

This program swaps the two steering numbers. Over the tape the robot now steers to the left, and over the floor it steers to the right.

Mission · 34 minutes

The winding aisle

The warehouse has a new aisle with a bend in it, marked by a line of tape on the floor. The cart has to follow the line round the bend to the stop bar without losing it. Then the warehouse wants it faster.

Two numbers set how the cart follows: how hard it steers and how fast it goes. Change one of them per run.

The mission from above: a line of tape with one bend, ending at the stop bar.

  • Check on the reference robot that the two colour sensors sit far enough apart for one to be over the tape while the other is over the floor. If they do not, this session needs a wider gap or a different stop.
