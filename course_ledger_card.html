Ledger card

One stamp a session, written by hand, in the student's own words.

How to use it

  • Print one card for each student before session 1 and keep the stack in the room.
  • Two minutes at the end of each session. The student writes, not the teacher.
  • The card comes out again in the next session's opener.

When something goes wrong

What you call itWhat it looks likeCheck first
Wrong wayThe robot moves backward when the program says forward.The port map. The two large motors' cables may be swapped.
No goThe program stops and the robot does not move.The robot check list, then capitals and await on the move lines.
CurveThe robot curves when the steering is 0.A cable rubbing a wheel, or a wheel not pushed fully onto its axle.
Runs pastThe robot does not stop at the line.Is DARK above the tape reading? Is the port letter right?
Stops at onceThe robot stops as soon as it starts.Is DARK below the floor reading?
