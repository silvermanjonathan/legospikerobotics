Course home

SPIKE Prime with Python, grades 4 and 5. Part 1 is sessions 1 to 4. Part 2 is sessions 5 to 8, for a class that continues. The class robot is LEGO's Advanced Driving Base. LEGO's building instructions are linked, not copied.
