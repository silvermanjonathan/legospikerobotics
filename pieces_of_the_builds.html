The pieces of the builds

Every LEGO piece the three builds use, with its picture and its name.

How to read a card

Each card is one LEGO piece. This page has every piece that the three builds of this course use: the Advanced Driving Base, the Game Master, and the Grabbers. That is 61 pieces.

The name in large letters is a plain name for the class to say. Under it is the colour the piece has in these builds. The same shape can come in other colours in the box.

LEGO's name is the name LEGO gives the piece in its own parts lists, copied letter for letter. LEGO writes these names in a short way. A number followed by M is a length counted in holes, so a 9M beam is a beam with 9 holes. CROSS means shaped like a plus sign, and W. or W/ means with. LEGO calls a peg a connector peg, and many builders call it a pin.

The labels at the bottom of a card name the builds that use the piece. Each picture is a drawing. A small piece is drawn larger than a big piece, so that its holes show.

Where each fact on this page comes from

  • Which pieces a build uses: read from LEGO's own building books on 13 November 2026, every page of all ten books. Pieces that look alike in the books, such as the black peg and the black axle peg, were read again at twice the size.
  • LEGO's names and numbers: LEGO's parts lists for the SPIKE Prime set, number 45678, and the SPIKE Prime Expansion Set, number 45681, as Brickset publishes them (45678, 45681). Misspellings such as CROSSALE are LEGO's own.
  • The names and numbers LEGO sells the hub, the motors, and the sensors under: LEGO Education's product list for SPIKE Prime. The hub battery is the Technic Large Hub Battery, number 45610.
  • The second check: each part number was looked up in the LDraw parts library, which names pieces on its own. The two names had to describe the same piece. Every piece and colour read from a book also had to be on the parts list of a set that build needs. All 61 were. The table below shows both names side by side.
  • The drawings: made by this course's build from the LDraw parts library, which its authors license under CC BY 4.0. They are not LEGO's pictures, and nothing from LEGO's building books is copied here.

What this page does not tell you

  • How many of each piece a build needs. LEGO's books show the count at every step.
  • The pieces in the sets that no build here uses, such as the distance sensor.

Which set a piece comes in

  • The Game Master and the Grabbers are each built from the SPIKE Prime set alone.
  • The Advanced Driving Base needs both sets. Its second large motor and its second colour sensor come from the Expansion Set. So do 7 pieces that are not in the SPIKE Prime set at all: cross block, 2 long (black), cross block, 3 by 2 (grey), round spacer, 1 long (black), long axle peg (red), axle peg (blue), axle with a stop, 5 long (brown), big wheel (black with a light blue tyre). The red long peg with a stop is in the Expansion Set only.
