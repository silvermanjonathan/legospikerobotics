Question: What does the colour sensor measure?

Reflected light: how much of its own light comes back from the floor, as a number from 0 to 100.

Question: Three lines move the robot until the line. What does each of the three do?

The first starts the wheels. The second waits until the reflected light is less than DARK. The third stops the wheels.

Question: Why does DARK have to be between your floor reading and your tape reading?

Then the test is false over the floor, so the hub keeps waiting, and true over the tape, so the hub goes on to the stop line.

Question: Your teacher moves the far line. Why does the robot still stop at it?

The program waits for a reading from the sensor. It does not count a distance.

Chunk 1 of 2

Read the whole program before it runs

This program has two parts. Lines 17 to 19 move the robot until the far line. Lines 22 and 23 turn it and move it forward.

Lines 6 to 9 hold every number the program depends on. Put your own values on lines 6, 7, and 9 before you run it.

from hub import port
import color_sensor
import motor_pair
import runloop

LINE_SENSOR = port.B
DARK = 50
FORWARD = 360
QUARTER_TURN = 180


async def main():
    # The left drive motor is in port A. The right drive motor is in port E.
    motor_pair.pair(motor_pair.PAIR_1, port.A, port.E)

    # Move until the far line.
    motor_pair.move(motor_pair.PAIR_1, 0, velocity=250)
    await runloop.until(lambda: color_sensor.reflection(LINE_SENSOR) < DARK)
    motor_pair.stop(motor_pair.PAIR_1)

    # Turn a quarter turn to the right, then move into the drop zone.
    await motor_pair.move_for_degrees(motor_pair.PAIR_1, QUARTER_TURN, 100, velocity=250)
    await motor_pair.move_for_degrees(motor_pair.PAIR_1, FORWARD, 0, velocity=250)


runloop.run(main())

Type the whole program. Take your time on line 18 and check it against your ledger card.

Mission · 30 minutes

The delivery run

Your robot is the cart that makes the last delivery of the day. It leaves the start line, finds the far line, turns, and parks in the drop zone. The far line is somewhere new on every run, and during the demo a visitor chooses where it goes.

First, plan. There are at least two ways to end in the drop zone: turn right and move forward, or turn left and move backward. Write both plans down, pick the one your team thinks will pass more often, and say why.

Demo · 20 minutes

Demo for families

Each team has about four minutes. Decide who says what before your turn.

  1. One builder says what the mission is, in one or two sentences.
  2. A visitor puts the far line somewhere between the two marks. The team runs the program. Do this up to 3 times.
  3. Each builder says which part of the robot they built in session 1.
  4. Each builder points at one line of the program and says what it does.
  5. If a run fails, say what you would change first, and which one thing you would change.
